Subscriptions renew quietly. Tally tells you three days before they do.

A plain list of everything you pay for monthly or yearly, with the next charge date next to each one. For anyone who has found a €9.99 line on a bank statement and couldn't remember signing up.

Tally keeps your list on your phone only: there is no account, no server, and no email or Telegram message sent on your behalf — reminders are ordinary notifications from the app itself.

How it works

  1. Add what you pay for

    Type the name, the price and the day it renews. Most people have between six and fifteen. It takes about five minutes to enter them all.

  2. Tally works out the dates

    Monthly, yearly, every four weeks, free trial ending on the 14th — Tally calculates every upcoming charge and keeps a running monthly total in your currency.

  3. You get a notice before each charge

    Three days ahead by default, or whatever you choose. That is enough time to cancel something you no longer use.

Two screens, mostly

The list shows what you pay and when. The upcoming view shows the next thirty days in order, so the month ahead is never a surprise.

What you get

People who add everything usually find one or two subscriptions they had forgotten about. For a typical list, that is somewhere around

€150–300 /year

A reminder before every charge
Local notifications, timed by you. They work in airplane mode.
One honest monthly total
Yearly and weekly plans are converted to a monthly figure. Different currencies are kept separate unless you set a rate.
Trials that don't slip through
Mark a subscription as a trial and Tally tells you the day before it starts charging.
Your data, in a file you own
Export to CSV at any time. Back up through your phone's own backup, or not at all.
No bank connection
Tally never asks for your bank login. You enter subscriptions yourself, which is also why nothing needs to leave the device.
